I've spent more than 20 years in the Singapore Armed Forces accumulating experience in running large scale operations, product and project management, as well as charting strategy and transformation for the organisation. In my career, I've had the privilege to upskill myself. I've completed two Master Degrees, at Florida State University and at Singapore Management University. With luck, I've had the opportunity to intern at a Deep Tech Venture firm and build life projects that I am proud to call my own. Today, I head the Army Safety Technology and Research Office, where I integrate emerging and mature technologies to enhance the safety and well being of over 200,000 people.

About

Living life, one step at a time.

I did not start with much. I was working odd jobs by thirteen to help with the finances at home. While I was never the strongest student, I've been blessed to be awarded multiple SAF scholarships. This offered me opportunities for life-long learning, expanded thinking and considered perspectives. I learned early that where you start does not decide where you finish.

My twenty years in the SAF have taught me how to be comfortable with ambiguity, gather very different views, and turn them into decisions people could act on. My career did not move in a straight line. While the peaks were pleasant, the troughs were where I've gained the most life lessons.

Taking a gap year in 2025 was one of the best decisions of my life. I went all in to rediscover my identity. I completed SMU's Master of Entrepreneurship and Innovation, scouted deep-tech deals as a Venture Creator at Origgin Ventures, and started my life coaching practice. Now, I'm back to the Singapore Army with renewed purpose, leading the Army Safety Technology and Research Office.

Beyond work

What keeps me curious.

🍷
Wine & whisky

I started collecting whisky in my mid-twenties, then fell into the world of wine. I've run several cask projects, collaborating with a local artist who designs each label from the inspiration he draws while tasting the liquid. Today I curate and host wine dinners to share my cellar with fellow enthusiasts. My only regret is not being able to sample every wine and whisky known to mankind.

📷
Arts

Photography, music and cooking. I started shooting digital and moved to 35mm and medium-format film; framing a scene trains the eye to be patient and present, and I love sealing a moment away forever. Piano and guitar let me slip into another dimension of an otherwise hectic life. Cooking is process too: thinking through a dish and finding ways to perfect it resonates with how I live.

📚
Reading

My love for reading only started in my late twenties. Since then I've devoured many books, non-fiction only. I love reading about decision-making and cognition, how the world works, business and investing. Lately I've turned to philosophy, which I find expands the breadth of one's thinking.

💰
Dividend investing

Cashflow is king. I developed a keen interest in building income through dividend investing, and over many years of deploying capital I've come to my own heuristic that turns it into a game I can keep playing. I'm also a strong advocate of Singapore's CPF system: I think it is the first and easiest gateway to learn the power of risk-free compounding and to build a financial safety net through disciplined top-ups and contributions.

🏃
Physical fitness

Healthy heart, healthy mind, healthy life, all anchored on physical movement. I move to live. I run around 25km and strength-train three times a week, not to impress anyone but to show what consistency can do for an ordinary person. Health, to me, is a lifestyle for the decades ahead, not a performance.

🌱
Personal growth

Introspection is something I value a lot in life. I believe that building the habit of self-reflection strengthens the muscle of self-awareness, and that is the first step to a better life.
